### 🦈 [DeadLine](https://github.com/Class1c22/DeadLine) · [▶ Play in browser](https://class1c09.itch.io/deadline)
Co-op multiplayer survival game made in Unity 6. A shark is eating your island, so you fish and feed it before it's gone.

- Multiplayer with **Photon PUN 2** (master-client authority, synced island deformation, voice chat)
- **Procedural island** mesh that is physically bitten away at runtime
- **Shader Graph** water and a GPU ripple simulation
- Fishing system with a Verlet-rope line, oxygen system, inventory, full menu and UI flow
- Shipped as a WebGL build on itch.io

### [CarGame-Android](https://github.com/Class1c22/CarGame-Android)
Casual Android game made in Unity 6 (URP). Your car drives forward with a mounted turret, shoots enemies, and you survive through an HP system to reach the finish.

- Gameplay loop with HP, win/lose screens and an ad screen
- Distance progress UI (bar with a car icon and live score)
- Hand-made stickman animations in Blender (idle, run, randomized hit reactions)
- Death effect: the enemy shatters into fragments with Unity physics
